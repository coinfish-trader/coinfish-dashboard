Personal tool for Coinfish Trading Company. Headlines aggregated from Yahoo Finance, CNBC, MarketWatch, and Federal Reserve RSS; filings and insider activity from SEC EDGAR (real-time filings feed + full-text search, cross-referenced against SEC's public ticker/CIK map to flag watchlist names); calendar data from Nasdaq public endpoints and the ForexFactory weekly economic feed; VIX, VIX9D, Treasury yields, and sector ETFs via yfinance; Fear & Greed Index via CNN's public data feed. All free, keyless sources. No Benzinga / PR Newswire / X monitoring feed, no standalone live options put/call ratio (CBOE's public CSV archives are stale; the Fear & Greed index's "Put/Call Options" component is the closest live proxy). For informational use only, not investment advice.
